# Space Skyboxes

Author: StumpyStrust; made with SpaceScape.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/space-skyboxes-0
Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/bkg.zip
License: CC0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Included skies: blue, lightblue and red (bkg1), six cube faces each.
Image pixels are unchanged. Unity imports at a maximum of 2048 pixels per face.
Left and right faces are swapped for Unity's Skybox/6 Sided convention, following
the Unity usage note on the source page. Repeat wrap is disabled to avoid seams.
Materials are configured by the editor importer after texture import.
The existing SampleScene skybox is preserved and is not replaced by this set.
